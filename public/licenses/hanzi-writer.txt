Stroke graphics are from hanzi-writer-data, which packages data from
Make Me a Hanzi (https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi). Those outlines
derive from fonts released under the Arphic Public License. See
https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi/blob/master/LICENSE.txt

Mandarin Daily bundles stroke data for characters used in HSK 1–2 and
loads other characters from jsDelivr (hanzi-writer-data@2.0.1), cached by
the service worker after the first fetch.
